# Common Cyber Attacks

Cyber attacks are attempts to compromise the confidentiality, integrity,
or availability of systems, networks, applications, or information.

## Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering technique where an attacker attempts to
trick a person into revealing information, opening a malicious attachment,
or visiting a malicious website.

Common examples include:

- Fake login pages
- Fraudulent emails
- Fake account notifications
- Malicious links

### Basic Protection

- Verify unexpected messages.
- Avoid suspicious links.
- Check the sender carefully.
- Use multi-factor authentication.
- Report suspicious messages.

## Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to perform unauthorized or harmful
actions.

Examples include:

- Viruses
- Worms
- Trojans
- Ransomware
- Spyware

### Basic Protection

- Keep software updated.
- Use security software.
- Avoid untrusted downloads.
- Maintain backups.
- Do not open suspicious attachments.

## Brute-Force Attacks

A brute-force attack attempts to gain access to an account by repeatedly
trying possible passwords or credentials.

### Basic Protection

- Use strong passwords.
- Use unique passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Implement appropriate login controls.
- Monitor authentication activity.

## Denial-of-Service

A Denial-of-Service (DoS) attack attempts to make a service or system
unavailable to legitimate users.

A Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attack uses multiple sources to
generate traffic toward a target.

### Basic Protection

Organizations can use:

- Traffic monitoring
- Rate limiting
- Network filtering
- Redundant infrastructure
- DDoS protection services

## Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating people into performing actions
or revealing information that could compromise security.

Examples include:

- Phishing
- Impersonation
- Pretexting
- Baiting

## Conclusion

Understanding common attacks helps security professionals recognize
suspicious activity and apply appropriate defensive measures.

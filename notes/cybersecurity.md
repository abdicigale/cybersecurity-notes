# Cybersecurity Fundamentals

## What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications,
devices, and information from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or
disruption.

Cybersecurity is important because organizations depend on digital systems
to store information, communicate, provide services, and conduct business.

## The CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a basic model used to describe three important goals of
information security.

### Confidentiality

Confidentiality means protecting information from unauthorized access.

Examples include:

- Password protection
- Access control
- Encryption
- Multi-factor authentication

### Integrity

Integrity means protecting information from unauthorized modification.

Examples include:

- File integrity monitoring
- Hashing
- Access controls
- Digital signatures

### Availability

Availability means making sure authorized users can access systems and
information when they need them.

Examples include:

- Backups
- Redundant systems
- Disaster recovery
- System monitoring

## Why Cybersecurity Matters

Cybersecurity helps organizations reduce the risk of:

- Data breaches
- Account compromise
- Malware infections
- Service disruption
- Unauthorized access
- Financial losses

## Basic Security Practices

Some basic cybersecurity practices include:

1. Use strong and unique passwords.
2. Enable multi-factor authentication.
3. Keep software updated.
4. Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
5. Back up important information.
6. Use appropriate access controls.
7. Monitor systems for suspicious activity.

## Conclusion

Cybersecurity is a continuous process. Organizations need people,
processes, and technology to identify threats, protect systems, detect
suspicious activity, respond to incidents, and recover from security events.
